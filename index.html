Why clean your panels every 6–12 months?

Solar panels only make power from the sunlight that actually reaches them. Anything sitting on the glass is energy you paid for and aren't getting.

Grime blocks the sun

Dust, pollen, salt spray and bird droppings build up into a film on the glass. Rain alone doesn't wash it away — it often just spreads it around and leaves residue.

Florida builds it up fast

Coastal salt air, heavy pollen seasons and humidity mean panels here get dirty faster than in drier climates, and the buildup bakes on in the heat.

Protect your investment

Clean panels produce closer to what they were rated for, and regular cleaning keeps buildup from staining the glass and frames over time.

Why Florida Homes Need Power Washing

Protect your investment from Florida's harsh environment

Salt Air Corrosion

Living near the coast means constant salt buildup on every exterior surface. Salt strips away protective finishes, traps moisture, and accelerates deterioration of wood, stucco, and metal. Properties within 3 miles of the ocean face accelerated damage.

Humidity & Moisture

Florida's 70%+ humidity year-round creates the perfect breeding ground for mold, mildew, and algae. These organisms don't just look bad—they actively deteriorate your home's surfaces, causing permanent staining and structural damage.

Intense UV & Heat

Florida's intense sunshine and heat accelerate the breakdown of paint, sealants, and protective coatings. Combined with moisture and organic growth, surfaces deteriorate faster than anywhere else in the country.

Preventative Maintenance Saves Money

Regular power washing isn't just cosmetic—it's preventative maintenance that protects your investment. Without routine cleaning, mold and algae become corrosive, causing wood to rot, paint to fail, and surfaces to degrade. A single cleaning can prevent thousands in repairs.

Our Cleaners

Best-in-Class Simple Green, Picked for Florida

Salt air, heat and humidity call for the right chemistry, not just more pressure. We run Simple Green on every job. Here's why.

Stucco, shutters, windows & doors

Simple Green Oxy Solve House & Siding

EPA Safer Choice certified, biodegradable and oxygen-powered. It lifts mildew, algae and black streaks off stucco so we can rinse gently instead of blasting your walls.

Driveways, pavers & pool decks

Simple Green Oxy Solve Concrete & Driveway

Biodegradable and made for porous concrete and brick pavers. It gets down into the surface to pull out the grime and dark staining that Florida rain leaves behind.

Salt film near the coast

Wash, Then a Fresh-Water Rinse

Salt dissolves in water. We wash glass, aluminum shutters and light fixtures with Simple Green, then rinse everything thoroughly with fresh water so no salt is left behind.

Why it matters here

  • Humidity keeps walls damp, so mildew and algae grow back fast. You need a cleaner that lifts the growth, not just the dirt.
  • Salt air leaves a film that holds moisture and eats at metal. A real wash and a full fresh-water rinse take it off.
  • Florida heat dries soap fast. We wet surfaces first and work in the shade or the morning so nothing dries on your glass.
  • Runoff goes to the river. Jupiter's storm drains lead to the Loxahatchee and the Intracoastal, so we choose cleaners made to be safer when they wash away.

The results you'll see

  • Black streaks and mildew lifted off the stucco, not just blasted around.
  • Clear windows and doors without salt haze or soap spots.
  • Shutters and fixtures rinsed free of corrosive salt.
  • Driveways and pavers back to their real color.
  • Your plants and lawn protected: we soak them before and rinse them after.

Really stubborn black mildew sometimes needs a stronger, diluted soft-wash treatment. If your home needs it, we'll tell you first, and we protect your landscaping either way.
